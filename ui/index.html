Hyperrhyme Matrix Technical Auditor

Book 3 defines a **Hyperrhyme** as a multisyllabic rhyme matching across 3 or more syllables ($\ge 3$). Audit candidate hyperrhymes against the three criteria of the Hyperrhyme Matrix.

1. Metrical Fidelity

Do both segments preserve natural speech stress inside the anapestic grid?

PASS (4 Syllables, Primary Stress Syl 1)

2. Acoustic Precision

Do syllables 1, 2, 3, and 4 maintain matching vowel and consonant phonemes?

PASS (Tight Near-Rhyme Match /dʒəs-li/)

3. Semantic Variation

Do the two rhyming elements belong to distinct semantic domains?

PASS (Altruism vs. Valor)

Matrix Evaluation Result: VALID HYPERRHYME (4 Syllables)

Chronometric System Breakdown: Phoneme sequence matching across `/dʒɛn-ə-rəs-li/` and `/kə-reɪ-dʒəs-li/`. Both elements satisfy metrical fidelity, acoustic precision, and semantic variation.
