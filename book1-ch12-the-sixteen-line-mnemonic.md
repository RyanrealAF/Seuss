### 12.2 Dual-Coding Theory and the Sonic GPS

**Established theory: Dual-Coding Theory (Paivio 1971).** Dual-Coding Theory holds that information is processed and stored in long-term memory through two distinct subsystems: a verbal system (non-verbal/linguistic representations) and a non-verbal/sensorimotor system (visual, acoustic, or motor representations). When information is encoded through both channels, retrieval paths are doubled, dramatically increasing recall probability. `[CITATION NEEDED: Paivio Dual-Coding Theory 1971]`

The predictable temporal grid acts as a spatial map in working memory:

### 12.3 Resolving Source-Issue I-09: Auditing the Source Mnemonic

In the original source files, a 16-line summary mnemonic verse was drafted to synthesize the book's core principles. However, a rigorous scansion audit reveals significant metrical errors in lines 5, 8, and 14, creating **Source-Issue I-09** (Series Bible §8).

#### The Scansion Audit of the Source Mnemonic:

1. **Line 5 Defect (11 Syllables / Lexical Stress Distortion):**
   - *Source Line 5:* `"The digest of DATA is EASY and FAST"`
   - *Audit:* Scans as `x / x x / x x / x x /` (11 syllables). The noun *digest* is naturally stressed on syllable 1 (*DI-gest*), but the source line forces demotion onto *di-* and stress onto *-gest*, distorting natural speech.
2. **Line 8 Defect (13 Syllables / Forced Peon Foot):**
   - *Source Line 8:* `"In a PRE-DICT-A-BLE PAT-TERN that CE-LE-BRATES the PAST"`
   - *Audit:* 13 syllables. The 4-syllable word *predictable* forces a 4-syllable foot (peon `x x x /`), causing a severe metrical stumble.
3. **Line 14 Defect (13 Syllables / Typo Disfluency):**
   - *Source Line 14:* `"With LES-LESS-ONS that TE-ACH the MIND how to LAST"`
   - *Audit:* Contains a duplicate word typo (*LES lessons*), creating 13 syllables and ruining the foot boundary.

### 12.7 Common Mistakes

**1. Distorting Natural Lexical Stress in Mnemonics:** Forcing stress onto the wrong syllable of a technical term (e.g., forcing *AN-a-tom-y* to scan as *an-a-TOM-y*).

**2. Allowing Extra Syllables to Creep In:** Failing to audit lines for 13-syllable peon foot glitches.

**3. Relying Solely on Rhyme Without Meter:** Writing rhyming lines with erratic syllable counts, destroying the Sonic GPS coordinate.
