## 3. The Technical Hyperrhyme Matrix Audit (Chronometric Verse)

For every Hyperrhyme example, the exact segment breakdown is recorded:

```
[Line 1]: With a CHRO-NO-MET-ric SYS-tem in a GEN-er-ous-ly GOV-erned ZONE
- Target Hyperrhyme Segment: /dʒɛn-ə-rəs-li/ (generously)
- Syllable Count: 4 Syllables
- Phoneme Matching: /dʒɛn/ + /ə/ + /rəs/ + /li/
- Stress Pattern: Primary stress on syllable 1 (/dʒɛn/), light run-up tail
- Metrical Position: Foot 4 run-up + stress
- Status: BASELINE HYPERRHYME MATCH

[Line 2]: We are COUR-age-ous-ly STAND-ing where the DAN-ger-ous-ly DARK was SHOWN
- Target Hyperrhyme Segment 1: /kə-reɪ-dʒəs-li/ (courageously)
- Target Hyperrhyme Segment 2: /deɪn-dʒə-rəs-li/ (dangerously)
- Phoneme Matching: Tight 4-syllable acoustic match with /dʒəs-li/ suffix closure
- Semantic Relationship: Distinct domains (valor vs threat)
- Status: VERIFIED EXACT/TIGHT HYPERRHYME PAIR

[Line 3]: Though the CAL-cu-lat-ing POW-ers of a VITAL AD-MIN-IS-TRA-TION CALL
- Target Segment: /kæl-kjə-leɪ-tɪŋ/ (calculating) vs /æd-mɪn-ɪ-streɪ-ʃən/ (administration)
- Stress Shift Audit: cal-cu-LA-ting vs ad-MIN-is-TRA-tion
- Status: EXPLICIT LOOSE NEAR-RHYME MATCH (Issue I-05) - Disgraded stress alignment noted!

[Line 4]: We can ELE-VATE the MEAS-URE till the SHAD-OWS of the FOE shall FALL
- Target Segment: /ɛl-ə-veɪt/ (elevate) / /mɛʒ-ər/ (measure)
- Status: BASELINE METRICAL RESOLUTION - Terminal rhyme /fɔːl/ matches /kɔːl/ in Line 3.
```
