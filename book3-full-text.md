### Chapter 2: The Hyperrhyme Matrix: Three Requirements
#### 2.1 Defining the Hyperrhyme
A **Hyperrhyme** is a multi-syllabic rhyme with exact or near-exact acoustic match across three or more syllables ($\ge 3$ syllables).
#### 2.2 The Three Requirements of the Matrix
The **Hyperrhyme Matrix** requires three simultaneous criteria:
1. **Metrical Fidelity:** The hyperrhyme must fit the established metrical grid without forcing unnatural stress.
2. **Acoustic Precision:** Syllables 1, 2, and 3 must maintain matching vowel and consonant features.
3. **Semantic Variation:** The rhyming words must belong to distinct semantic domains to avoid tautology.
#### 2.3 Theory: Matrix Evaluation Mechanics
Evaluating hyperrhyme chains against working memory limits.
#### 2.4 Worked Examples: Matrix Scoring Table
Auditing *generously / courageously* against the 3 requirements.
#### 2.5 Exercise: Hyperrhyme Construction Drill
Construct a 3-syllable hyperrhyme pair meeting all three Matrix criteria.
#### 2.6 Common Mistakes
Sacrificing natural speech stress to force a 4-syllable match.
#### 2.7 Chapter Summary
The Hyperrhyme Matrix evaluates 3+ syllable rhymes for metrical fidelity, acoustic precision, and semantic variation.

### Chapter 6: Near-Rhyme and Acoustic Precision
#### 6.1 Exact vs. Slant / Near-Rhyme
Near-rhyme (slant rhyme) matches vowel sounds while varying consonants, expanding vocabulary range.
#### 6.2 Resolving Issue I-05: Critical Case Analysis
Resolving Source-Issue I-05: *generously / courageously* provides a strong stress-matched near-rhyme, whereas *calculating / vital administration* is a loose near-rhyme match due to stress shifts (cal-cu-LA-ting vs. ad-MIN-is-TRA-tion). Loose matches must be labeled as such.
#### 6.3 Theory: Feature Matching in Auditory Cortex
Phonetic feature overlap (vowel height, place of articulation) governs perceived rhyme strength.
#### 6.4 Worked Examples: Audit of Loose vs. Tight Near-Rhymes
Comparing tight stress-matched near-rhymes with loose stress-shifted matches.

#### 6.7 Chapter Summary
