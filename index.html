00 / THE SERIES

Start with a stable pulse.
Then learn how to break it.

The series calls the stable pattern the Anapestic Anchor: four anapests, normally twelve syllables, creating a recognizable rhythmic home. The work then studies what happens when that home is bent, compressed, layered, or deliberately violated.

The framework distinguishes established research from the series' own interpretive models. Claims that need verification remain marked in the working source files rather than being dressed up as settled science.

03 / QUICK GLOSSARY
Anapest
Two unstressed syllables followed by one stressed: x x /.
Anapestic Anchor
The series' name for anapestic tetrameter as a stable rhythmic home.
Metrical Mandate
No meaningful departure without an established baseline.
Metrical Hook
The memorable, retrievable quality of predictable meter.
Rhythmic Contract
The expectation a repeated pattern creates between performer and listener.
Hyperrhyme
Multi-syllabic rhyme spanning three or more syllables.
Syncopation
Emphasis placed where the pulse does not expect it.
Caesura
A deliberate pause inside a line.
