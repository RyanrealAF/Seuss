## 3. The Master 30/30/25/15 Assessment Rubric (Resolving Issue I-03)

| Criterion | Weight | Exemplary (Mastery) | Proficient | Developing / Disfluent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Metrical Fidelity & Grid Coherence** | **30%** | $RC_{foot} \ge 90\%$ and $RC_{grid} \ge 90\%$; clean scansion ladders and 16th grid tables; no unbuffered clashes. | $RC_{foot} \ge 80\%$; minor scansion errors; buffered variations. | $RC_{foot} < 80\%$; unbuffered stress clashes; irregular syllable clutter. |
| **2. Analytical Depth & Structural Control** | **30%** | Flawless Hyperrhyme Matrix score; clear load balancing; master audit logs. | Meets matrix criteria; minor load imbalances. | Incomplete matrix audit; excessive extraneous load. |
| **3. Portability & Cross-Genre Transfer** | **25%** | Seamless execution across Boom-Bap, Drill, and Trap styles; flawless adaptation log. | Successful adaptation across 2 subgenres; minor delivery hangups. | Inability to adapt skeleton across subgenres; cadence collapses. |
| **4. Equity, Accessibility & Ethical Reflection** | **15%** | Thorough ethical reflection on tool vs. ideology (Seuss legacy); full UDL alignment; zero slurs/caricatures. | Clear statement on ethics; respects UDL options. | Missing ethical reflection; uses improper attribution or harmful tropes. |
