## 3. Comparative Master Table (Three Subgenre Roles)

| Subgenre | Tempo / Groove | Anchor Functional Role | Key Structural Mechanism | Primary Emotion / Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Boom-Bap** | 85–95 BPM / Heavy Swing | **Stabilizer** | Strict acatalectic 12-syllable grid; on-beat downbeat landings | Nostalgic, authoritative, steady pocket groove |
| **Drill** | 140–145 BPM / Sliding Snares | **Subverter** | **Melancholic Meter** (monotone spondaic stress); weaponized satisfaction cycle | Threatening tension, stark contrast, cold detachment |
| **Trap** | 130–150 BPM / Triplet Hi-Hats | **Compressor** | Rapid syllable compression into 16th/32nd triplets; iambic pickups | Urgent, hyper-dense, kinetic energy |
